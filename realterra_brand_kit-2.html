Brand Positioning

Point of view

RealTerra exists for investors who want their real estate decisions underwritten the way a portfolio allocation would be — with a defined trajectory, exit window, and liquidity profile, not a lifestyle narrative. The brand should read first as an analytics practice, and second as a luxury one.

Every decision in this kit — the cooler pine over a warmer heritage green, the editorial serif over a classical one, the reserved use of colour inside data only — exists to keep that order intact.

Logo

Wordmark only.

The wordmark is the entire mark — no icon, no monogram, no symbol. One typeface, one weight, one colour at a time.

Clear space & minimum size

Maintain clear space on all sides equal to the cap-height of the "R." Do not scale the wordmark below 90px wide for digital, or 20mm for print.

Usage rules

The wordmark renders in a single colour only — Pine or Ink on light backgrounds, Paper on dark. Always Newsreader, always this weight. No drop shadows, outlines, gradients, or rotation.

Small-format placements

For app icons, favicons, and social avatars, set a single capital "R" in Newsreader, same weight and colour rules as the wordmark. No separate symbol is designed — typography carries the brand at every size.

Incorrect use

Do not recolour the wordmark, add a background shape or container behind it, stretch it off-ratio, or substitute any other typeface.

Typography

Three faces, three jobs.

A financial-editorial serif for authority, a clean grotesk for everything you read, and IBM Plex Mono reserved strictly for numerals.

Display — Newsreader, Semibold / Italic
Off-plan, quantified.
Headlines, report titles, hero statements, and the wordmark. Roman for authority, italic sparingly on a single word — never full sentences.
Body & UI — Inter, Regular / Semibold
Every property is underwritten against liquidity, exit window, and five-year trajectory before it reaches an investor.
All body copy, section labels, captions, buttons, navigation, icon labels. Semibold for emphasis — never italics, never mono.
Data — IBM Plex Mono

Numerals only: prices, percentages, dates, hex codes, chart values. Never section titles, never labels, never prose — that discipline is what makes the mono read as data and not decoration.
The one rule that governs this whole system: if it's a number, it's mono. If it's a word, it's Inter or Newsreader. No exceptions, no borderline cases — this is what was inconsistent in earlier drafts and is now fixed throughout.

Photography & Imagery

Architecture, not lifestyle.

Skyline, structure, and material — not stock portraiture. Every image is treated with a Pine-to-Ink gradient duotone at low opacity, with a fine diagonal linework overlay that echoes the trajectory icon.

Skyline / Wide
Facade Detail
Interior / Structural

Subject matter

Building facades, structural detail, skylines at dusk, construction/off-plan progress. Avoid people as the primary subject — if a person appears, they are secondary to the architecture, never a lifestyle model.

Treatment

Apply the Pine → Ink duotone gradient at 60–75% opacity over source imagery, plus the diagonal hairline overlay used in this kit, so every asset feels part of one system regardless of source photo.

Colour temperature

Cool, not warm. Avoid golden-hour amber tones that pull toward the heritage-luxury look this brand deliberately moved away from.

Never use

Stock fashion portraiture, gold-toned filters, motivational-poster text overlays, or any image where the property itself isn't the visible subject.

Voice & Tone

Write like an analyst, not a salesperson.

Four working rules for anyone writing captions, scripts, or report copy for RealTerra.

Number before narrative

Open with the data point, then explain it. "11.4% five-year appreciation in Dubai South" — not "Dubai South is booming."

Cite the source, every time

Every stat carries an attribution — DLD, Property Monitor, developer disclosure. An unsourced number is a claim, not a data point.

No superlatives without evidence

"Best," "guaranteed," and "hottest" are banned unless a specific figure backs the claim in the same sentence.

Address the allocation, not the lifestyle

Speak to yield, exit window, and liquidity — not to how a property will make someone feel.

CTA System

Named actions only.

Every CTA names the action and the asset — never a generic label.
